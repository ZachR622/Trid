  - icon: 🧱
    title: Shapes & meshes
    details: Cubes, rounded boxes, spheres, cylinders, tori and more, lathe and extrude, your own vertices, or models from .obj files.
    link: /shapes
  - icon: 🦊
    title: Modeling
    details: Model in code. Splines, sweeps, lofts, blobs that melt together, subdivision, bevels and deformers.
    link: /modeling/
  - icon: 💡
    title: Lights
    details: Ambient, directional and point lights in any color, as many as you like, with smooth or flat shading.
    link: /lights
  - icon: ✨
    title: Effects & post effects
    details: Toon, rim glow, gradients, fog or your own GLSL on any material. Bloom, vignette, color grading and grain on the whole picture.
    link: /effects
  - icon: 🖼️
    title: Textures
    details: Images, canvases and videos, or textures made in code (marble, wood, bricks…). Wrapped by shape, tiled, scrolled, or one per face.
    link: /textures
  - icon: 🎯
    title: Picking & rays
    details: Find the exact triangle under the mouse, cast rays through a scene, and test whether points are inside a mesh or how far they are from it.
    link: /modeling/queries
  - icon: 🎥
    title: Tools
    details: Orbit and fly cameras, move / rotate / scale gizmos, a view cube, dragging and hover highlight, one line each.
    link: /tools
  - icon: ⚡
    title: Canvas 2D or WebGL
    details: Draw with the plain canvas, or switch to WebGL for exact depth, per-pixel lighting and big models. Same scene, same code.
    link: /renderers
  - icon: 🎲
    title: Random & noise
    details: Seeded random numbers and smooth noise for rocks, terrain, wobble and anything that shouldn't look made by hand.
    link: /noise
  - icon: 💾
    title: Import & export
    details: Load .obj and .mtl files, save your models back as OBJ for Blender, take screenshots and record videos of the canvas.
    link: /export
  - icon: 📐
    title: Pure math
    details: Every transform, projection and matrix function is exported, so you can use the pipeline piece by piece.
    link: /api
